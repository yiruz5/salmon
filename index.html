Journey up the Cedar River

The Cedar River is home to many salmon. Every October, salmon travel upstream along the river to return to the place where they were born. They find mates, reproduce, and eventually die. Their bodies nourish the surrounding landscape and provide nutrients that help support the entire ecosystem.
Baby salmon born here will remember the scent of the river and find their way back years later.
